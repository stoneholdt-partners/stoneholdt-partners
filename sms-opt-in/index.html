SMS Opt-In

Last updated November 3, 2026

This page explains how you opt in to Stoneholdt Partners Text Updates, the text messaging program operated by Stoneholdt Partners LLC.

How you opt in

You opt in verbally, during a phone call with a Stoneholdt Partners LLC representative. Before sending you any text message, the representative reads this disclosure and asks for your agreement:

"Is it okay if Stoneholdt Partners LLC texts you at this number about this property and our conversation? Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not required to buy or sell. Our Privacy Policy and Terms are at stoneholdtpartners.com."

We text only people who say yes. We record the date, time, phone number and representative for each consent, and we send one confirmation text after you opt in.

What you will receive

One-to-one text messages about a property, offer or transaction you have discussed with us, such as scheduling, documents, follow-ups, closing details and information you asked for. We do not use this program for bulk marketing or cold outreach.

Frequency and costs

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for help, or email stoneholdt.acquisitions@gmail.com. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You may also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT. Reply START to start receiving messages again.

Your information

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing or selling any property or service.

Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
