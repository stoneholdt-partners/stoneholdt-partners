Stoneholdt Partners LLC ("Stoneholdt Partners," "we," "us") is a real estate company that buys residential properties and lots from property owners and works with builders and developers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, including how we handle text messages.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, for example when you speak with us by phone, send us an email or a text message, or fill out a form on our website. This can include:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and mailing address.
  • Details about a property you own or are interested in, such as its address and condition, and your goals and timeline.
  • The content of our conversations with you, including calls, emails and text messages, and notes we make about them.
  • Records related to a transaction, such as contracts, escrow and title documents.

We may also receive information about properties from public sources, such as county property records.

How we use your information

  • To communicate with you about a property, an offer, a transaction or a business opportunity.
  • To prepare, manage and complete real estate transactions, including working with title and escrow companies.
  • To keep records, follow up on conversations you have asked us to continue, and improve how we serve you.
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights.

Text messaging

We send text messages only to people who have given us permission to text them, by checking the SMS consent box on our contact form or by agreeing during a phone call with one of our representatives, as described on our SMS Opt-In page. Messages are one-to-one and relate to the property, offer or transaction we discussed, such as scheduling, documents and updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or sale.

Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business and complete the work you have asked us to do:

  • With service providers that help us operate, such as telephone and text messaging, email, hosting and record-keeping providers, who may use it only to provide those services to us.
  • With title companies, escrow agents, attorneys and other parties to a transaction you are part of.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Stoneholdt Partners, our customers or others.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet legal, tax and record-keeping requirements.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure.

Your choices

  • To stop text messages, reply STOP to any message.
  • To ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop other communications, email us at the address below.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
